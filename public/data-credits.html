COMMON TABLE · DATA CREDITS

The sources behind your evening.

Weather

Weather data and forecasts are supplied by Open-Meteo under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0). See the source attribution information and forecast documentation.

When the primary weather service is rate limited or unavailable, forecasts may be supplied by the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway) through Locationforecast, under CC BY 4.0. See the MET Norway licensing policy. Missing feels-like temperatures, precipitation values and daylight information are left unknown.

Common Table rounds requested coordinates to two decimal places, formats and rounds temperature and wind values for display, translates documented condition codes into readable labels, converts UTC forecasts to the area’s local timezone, and shows a forecast only when the selected hour is actually available. Wind values supplied in metres per second are converted to kilometres per hour. No missing forecast hours are interpolated. These are display changes; the underlying forecast values come from the credited source.

Forecasts are estimates. Available dates vary by the forecast horizon. Missing hours are shown as unavailable; current conditions are never substituted for a missing dinner forecast. Saved weather is labelled when stale and expires after two hours.

Maps

Geographic map data are © OpenStreetMap contributors under the Open Database Licence. Vector map tiles and the base map style are supplied by OpenFreeMap. The map also displays the applicable attribution alongside its data.

The illustrated sample atlas and fictional sample restaurants are labelled separately in Common Table.
