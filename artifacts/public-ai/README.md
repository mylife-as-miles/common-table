# Public live intelligence verification

Published version 19 opens The Waiter and restaurant recommendations to signed-out visitors and other accounts. Real production checks confirmed an anonymous browser reply and eight model-ranked nearby places. The browser needed no sign-in or authorization header; its HttpOnly fair-use cookie was accepted, sent and hidden from JavaScript. Provider labels do not appear in the UI.

All 236 unit checks and 13 focused browser cases passed, including three accessibility scans. The earlier focused test assumption about the Explore destination was corrected and its initial report retained. App/server type checks, targeted lint and production build passed. The focused client build and published source differ only in verifier/test changes.

Native public ingress canaries confirmed that forged identity headers do not create a signed-in identity and anonymous visitors cannot read private shared tables. Weather and dinner forecast also returned HTTP 200. Invalid questions and cross-origin requests were rejected before generation. These no-paid canaries are separate from the two explicitly authorized actual generation checks.

The original global allowance still caps reservations at $5. Its ledger rose from 20 cents across four earlier attempts to 30 cents across six total attempts. Reservations are not exact billing or an available account balance. Both public flows share five requests per visitor per UTC day and six globally per minute, with no automatic retry, refill, top-up or reload.

Anonymous cookie clearing can change a soft visitor marker; the immutable global ledger is the hard cost boundary. Quota counters need a retention policy before a long-lived rollout. Private tables still require trusted Sites identity and membership; guest quota markers never authenticate anyone. No private shared writes, invitations, bookings or GPS/location sharing occurred during these checks.
